# Sunbreaking
## Brandrobkus

This is a mod that is heavily derived from the Destiny series. It at current adds two new weapon types: the Throwing Hammer and the Glaive. It also adds three new Super Items: the Hammer of Sol, the Shadowshot Bow, and the Stormcaller's Bond. It also adds three new Armor sets: the Sunbreaker's set, the Nightstalker's set, and the Stormcaller's set. These Super Items and Armor sets can be equipped with up to two Fragments and Aspects respectively to create more unique build options that are otherwise not in the game. It is intended for modpacks that allow post-Netherite progression and introduce enemy types that are more suited for such.

While this mod will take creative liberties in how certain items, effect, and abilities from the game are portrayed, this is not in any way attempting to pass off the ideas of Bungie as my own. As more and more items get added to the mod, it will still adhere to the familiar abilities from the game, but will have a more creative liberties so to not copy or plagiarize the Destiny devs.

This mod will infrequently update, but updates are planned, so please be patient while I learn the ins and outs of modding in everything I want to put in. This mod has been in the works on and off for almost a year, and it may not be much, but I am proud of how it turned out!
